VoltEdge – Portable Buck Converter Battery Charging System

Built in: 2026 (B.Tech. Second Year)

Designed and analyzed a high-efficiency portable battery charging system using LM2576 and LM2596 buck converter architectures for regulated 5V output from 9V–12V sources. Instead of using conventional linear regulators such as the 7805, the system was intentionally built using switching buck converter topology because of its significantly higher efficiency, lower heat generation, and superior energy utilization. The project included complete LM2576 circuit design, breadboard prototyping, KiCad schematic and PCB layout design, and manual soldering/manufacturing of the converter on a zero PCB board by hand. Although the soldered LM2576 implementation was unsuccessful, the process provided extensive experience in hardware debugging, PCB-level manufacturing, and power electronics analysis. Following this, a detailed experimental analysis of the LM2596 buck converter module was performed through multiple tests including line regulation, load regulation, ripple voltage analysis, efficiency measurements, and lithium-ion battery charging tests to evaluate voltage stability, charging behavior, and converter performance in portable power applications.

SmartMediBot – Automatic Pill Dispenser

Built in: 2025 (B.Tech. First Year)

Designed and implemented a smart medication dispensing system using Arduino for automated time-based pill delivery. The setup integrates a servo motor for precision-controlled pill release, an LCD interface showing live time and alerts, and audio-visual indicators (buzzer + LED) to notify users. It helps ensure medication adherence and reduces human error, especially for elderly and chronic patients. Serial monitoring was used for debugging and system feedback.

BlueRover – Bluetooth-Controlled Exploration Vehicle

Built in: 2021 (9th Grade)

Built a Bluetooth-controlled robotic vehicle capable of navigating remote or hazardous terrains through smartphone control. The system can be expanded with cameras, sensors, GPS, and mapping modules for applications such as surveillance, rescue operations, and environmental exploration. The modular architecture enables future transition from manual to autonomous navigation and data collection.

PowerStride – Energy Generating Smart Shoes

Built in: 2023 (11th Grade)

Developed energy-harvesting smart footwear using piezoelectric sensors to convert walking energy into storable electrical power inside a rechargeable Li-ion battery. The system also integrates ultrasonic obstacle detection for visually impaired users, triggering buzzer alerts upon detecting nearby obstacles. The project combines sustainable energy generation with assistive embedded technology in a wearable form.

AgroSense – Integrated Smart Farming Device Suite

Built in: 2022 (10th Grade)

Engineered a multi-functional agricultural monitoring and automation system combining smart irrigation, fire and gas detection, environmental monitoring, and rain-water management subsystems. The setup uses multiple sensors and relay-based automation to improve water management, crop protection, environmental awareness, and smart agricultural efficiency.

  • Automatic soil moisture-based irrigation control
  • Fire and gas leak detection with alarms
  • Temperature and humidity monitoring using LCD display
  • Rain and water-level responsive automation
